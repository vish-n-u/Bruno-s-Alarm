BRUNO'S ALARM — PRIVACY POLICY
Effective September 6, 2026
Package: com.brunosalarm.app

Bruno's Alarm wakes you up with a real dog's twice-daily howl. This page explains,
plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

THE SHORT VERSION
There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no server this app talks to. Every
alarm you schedule is stored only on your own phone. The one exception is the video
panel, which embeds YouTube — see Section 4.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
None. Bruno's Alarm has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no first-party backend
server. We don't know who you are, and we have no way to find out.

2. WHAT'S STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
Everything the app needs lives entirely on your phone, never transmitted anywhere:
- Your scheduled alarm times, held by Android's own AlarmManager.
- Whether you've completed the app's one-time onboarding screen (a single yes/no
  flag, saved locally via Android's standard app storage).
Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Nothing survives anywhere else.

3. PERMISSIONS THIS APP REQUESTS, AND WHY
Android requires apps to declare each permission up front. Here's what Bruno's
Alarm asks for and the exact reason:

- POST_NOTIFICATIONS
  Lets the app show you anything at all — required on Android 13+ for any
  notification, including the alarm itself.

- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
  Lets Bruno's alarm fire at the exact scheduled second, the same permission
  every alarm-clock app uses.

- USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT
  Lets the alarm screen wake your phone and show over the lock screen when it
  goes off — otherwise it would just be a quiet notification.

- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
  Lets your alarms survive a phone restart, so a reboot overnight doesn't
  silently cancel tomorrow's wake-up.

4. YOUTUBE EMBEDDED VIDEO
The app's video panel plays Bruno's clips through YouTube's standard embedded
player. When that player loads, Google may collect data according to its own
Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), the same as on any website
that embeds a YouTube video. Bruno's Alarm has no access to and receives none of
that data — it's a direct relationship between your device and Google.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Bruno's Alarm isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect
information from anyone, of any age — see Section 1.

6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
