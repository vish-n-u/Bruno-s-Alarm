BRUNO'S ALARM — PRIVACY POLICY
Effective September 21, 2026
Package: com.brunosalarm.app

Bruno's Alarm wakes you up with a real dog's twice-daily howl, and lets you chat with
other viewers while he's live. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and
doesn't do with your data.

THE SHORT VERSION
There's no sign-up and we never learn your name, email, or phone number. Your alarms
are stored only on your phone. When you use live chat, the app creates an anonymous
device ID (no personal info attached) so messages can be moderated and rate-limited —
anything you send in chat is public to everyone watching that session. We also use
Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics for basic usage and crash data, and Cloudflare to
deliver the video. No ads, nothing sold to third parties.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
- Automatically, via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: basic app-usage events
  (like which screens you open), device/OS info, and crash or error reports. This
  is standard, aggregate diagnostic data — it isn't tied to your name or contact
  info.
- If you use live chat: an anonymous device ID (created by Firebase Anonymous
  Authentication, with no name/email/phone attached), the text of any message you
  send, an auto-generated display name like "Viewer 4821", and a timestamp. These
  are stored in a shared database (Cloud Firestore) and are visible to everyone
  viewing that session's chat, since that's the point of a public chat.
- If you report a chat message: the message you reported, your reason, and your
  anonymous device ID, so we can review it.
- If you turn on the optional "Ring when Bruno goes live" alarm: Firebase Cloud
  Messaging gives your device a push identifier, and the app subscribes it to a
  "live" topic so it can be told when Bruno starts streaming. No name, email, or
  phone number is attached, and turning the option off unsubscribes it.
- On Android, Firebase Analytics may read your device's advertising ID (where your
  device provides one) to measure usage. We do not use it to show ads.
- We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, or precise
  location, and there is no account or sign-up anywhere in the app.

2. WHAT'S STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE
Never transmitted anywhere:
- Your scheduled alarm times, held by Android's own AlarmManager.
- Whether you've completed the app's one-time onboarding screen, and whether you
  turned on the optional live alarm.
- A nickname you can optionally type during onboarding — currently unused by any
  feature (live chat generates its own display name instead), kept only in case a
  future feature uses it.
- Whether you've accepted live chat's conduct rules, and a list of any chat
  participants you've chosen to block. Blocking is local only — it hides that
  person's messages on your device, it doesn't remove them for anyone else or
  notify them.
- The most recently cached copy of Bruno's howl, downloaded so the app has
  something to play even without a live connection.
Uninstalling the app removes all of this. Nothing survives anywhere else.

3. LIVE CHAT AND MODERATION
Chat is only open while Bruno is actually live, and resets with each session — it's
not one continuous room. Every message goes through a moderated server function
before it's posted: it checks length, filters certain language, and enforces a
rate limit and a per-session cap so chat can't be flooded. Messages that get posted
are public — anyone viewing that session can read them, and they're stored as part
of that session's chat history for moderation and app functionality. We don't sell
or share chat content with third parties, and it's never linked to your name, email,
or any account.

Two tools if something in chat bothers you:
- Report a message — sends it to us for review, along with your reason.
- Block a viewer — hides their messages for you, immediately, on your device only.

4. SERVICES WE USE
- Firebase (Google): Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions
  power live chat; Cloud Messaging delivers the optional live alerts; Analytics and
  Crashlytics provide basic usage and crash data.
  Governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Cloudflare Stream and R2: Stream delivers the live video, and R2 stores the saved
  recording your phone downloads for the alarm. Loading video or downloading the
  recording means your device's IP address and standard request data reach
  Cloudflare's servers, the same as loading anything from a CDN. Governed by
  Cloudflare's Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
- Vercel: hosts the small server the app asks for the link to Bruno's latest
  recording. Your device's IP address and standard request data reach it, and we do
  not keep them. Governed by Vercel's Privacy Policy:
  https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
None of these services are used to serve you ads, and Bruno's Alarm doesn't run any
ad network of its own.

5. PERMISSIONS THIS APP REQUESTS, AND WHY
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS — lets the app show you anything at all, required on
  Android 13+ for any notification, including the alarm itself.
- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM — let Bruno's alarm fire at the exact
  scheduled minute, the same permissions every alarm-clock app uses.
- USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — lets the alarm screen wake your phone and show over
  the lock screen when it goes off.
- WAKE_LOCK — keeps the phone awake while the alarm is ringing.
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — lets your alarms survive a phone restart.
- MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK —
  let the alarm sound play reliably, including while the app isn't in the
  foreground, and keep it audible while it rings.
- VIBRATE — lets the alarm vibrate your phone alongside the sound.
- INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — download Bruno's latest recording and play the
  live video.
- com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE — lets the optional live alerts
  reach your phone (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*) — declared
  by Firebase Analytics; not used to show ads.

The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos, files, or
location.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Bruno's Alarm isn't directed at children. Live chat's public, moderated nature
means it isn't intended for use by children either. We don't knowingly collect
information from children — if you believe a child has used chat inappropriately
or shared personal information, contact us using the email below and we'll remove
it.
