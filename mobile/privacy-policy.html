Bruno's Alarm — Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026 · com.brunosalarm.app

Bruno's Alarm wakes you up with a real dog's twice-daily howl, and lets you chat with other viewers while he's live. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

The short version

There's no sign-up and we never learn your name, email, or phone number. Your alarms are stored only on your phone. When you use live chat, the app creates an anonymous device ID (no personal info attached) so messages can be moderated and rate-limited — anything you send in chat is public to everyone watching that session. We also use Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics for basic usage and crash data, and Cloudflare to deliver the video. No ads, nothing sold to third parties.

1 Information we collect

  • Automatically, via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: basic app-usage events (like which screens you open), device/OS info, and crash or error reports. This is standard, aggregate diagnostic data — it isn't tied to your name or contact info.
  • If you use live chat: an anonymous device ID (created by Firebase Anonymous Authentication, with no name/email/phone attached), the text of any message you send, an auto-generated display name like Viewer 4821, and a timestamp. These are stored in a shared database (Cloud Firestore) and are visible to everyone viewing that session's chat, since that's the point of a public chat.
  • If you turn on the optional "Ring when Bruno goes live" alarm: Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a push identifier, and the app subscribes it to a "live" topic so it can be told when Bruno starts streaming. No name, email, or phone number is attached, and turning the option off unsubscribes it.
  • On Android, Firebase Analytics may read your device's advertising ID (where your device provides one) to measure usage. We do not use it to show ads.
  • If you report a chat message: the message you reported, your reason, and your anonymous device ID, so we can review it.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location, and there is no account or sign-up anywhere in the app.

2 What's stored only on your device

Never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your scheduled alarm times, held by Android's own AlarmManager.
  • Whether you've completed the app's one-time onboarding screen, and whether you turned on the optional live alarm.
  • A nickname you can optionally type during onboarding — currently unused by any feature (live chat generates its own display name instead), kept only in case a future feature uses it.
  • Whether you've accepted live chat's conduct rules, and a list of any chat participants you've chosen to block. Blocking is local only — it hides that person's messages on your device, it doesn't remove them for anyone else or notify them.
  • The most recently cached copy of Bruno's howl, downloaded for offline playback.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this. Nothing survives anywhere else.

3 Live chat and moderation

Chat is only open while Bruno is actually live, and resets with each session — it's not one continuous room. Every message goes through a moderated server function before it's posted: it checks length, filters certain language, and enforces a rate limit and a per-session cap so chat can't be flooded. Messages that get posted are public — anyone viewing that session can read them, and they're stored as part of that session's chat history for moderation and app functionality. We don't sell or share chat content with third parties, and it's never linked to your name, email, or any account.

Two tools if something in chat bothers you:

  • Report a message — sends it to us for review, along with your reason.
  • Block a viewer — hides their messages for you, immediately, on your device only.

4 Services we use

  • Firebase (Google): Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions power live chat; Cloud Messaging delivers the optional live alerts; Analytics and Crashlytics provide basic usage and crash data. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Stream and R2: Stream delivers the live video, and R2 stores the saved recording your phone downloads for the alarm. Loading video or downloading the recording means your device's IP address and standard request data reach Cloudflare's servers, the same as loading anything from a CDN. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel: hosts the small server the app asks for the link to Bruno's latest recording. Your device's IP address and standard request data reach it, and we do not keep them. Governed by Vercel's Privacy Policy.

None of these services are used to serve you ads, and Bruno's Alarm doesn't run any ad network of its own.

5 Permissions this app requests, and why

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS Lets the app show you anything at all — required on Android 13+ for any notification, including the alarm itself.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM · USE_EXACT_ALARM Let Bruno's alarm fire at the exact scheduled minute, the same permissions every alarm-clock app uses.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Lets the alarm screen wake your phone and show over the lock screen when it goes off.
  • WAKE_LOCK Keeps the phone awake while the alarm is ringing.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Lets your alarms survive a phone restart, so a reboot overnight doesn't silently cancel tomorrow's wake-up.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS · FOREGROUND_SERVICE · FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Let the alarm sound play reliably, including while the app isn't in the foreground, and keep it audible while it rings.
  • VIBRATE Lets the alarm vibrate your phone alongside the sound.
  • INTERNET · ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Download Bruno's latest recording and play the live video.
  • com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE Lets the optional live alerts reach your phone (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • AD_ID Declared by Firebase Analytics; not used to show ads.

The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos, files, or location.

6 Children's privacy

Bruno's Alarm isn't directed at children. Live chat's public, moderated nature means it isn't intended for use by children either. We don't knowingly collect information from children — if you believe a child has used chat inappropriately or shared personal information, contact us using the email below and we'll remove it.
