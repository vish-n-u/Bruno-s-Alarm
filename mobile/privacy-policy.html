Bruno's Alarm — Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · com.brunosalarm.app

Bruno's Alarm wakes you up with a real dog's twice-daily howl. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

The short version

There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no server this app talks to. Every alarm you schedule is stored only on your own phone. The one exception is the video panel, which embeds YouTube — see Section 4.

1 Information we collect

None. Bruno's Alarm has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no first-party backend server. We don't know who you are, and we have no way to find out.

2 What's stored on your device

Everything the app needs lives entirely on your phone, never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your scheduled alarm times, held by Android's own AlarmManager.
  • Whether you've completed the app's one-time onboarding screen (a single yes/no flag, saved locally via Android's standard app storage).

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Nothing survives anywhere else.

3 Permissions this app requests, and why

Android requires apps to declare each permission up front. Here's what Bruno's Alarm asks for and the exact reason:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS Lets the app show you anything at all — required on Android 13+ for any notification, including the alarm itself.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Lets Bruno's alarm fire at the exact scheduled second, the same permission every alarm-clock app uses.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Lets the alarm screen wake your phone and show over the lock screen when it goes off — otherwise it would just be a quiet notification.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Lets your alarms survive a phone restart, so a reboot overnight doesn't silently cancel tomorrow's wake-up.

4 YouTube embedded video

The app's video panel plays Bruno's clips through YouTube's standard embedded player. When that player loads, Google may collect data according to its own Privacy Policy, the same as on any website that embeds a YouTube video. Bruno's Alarm has no access to and receives none of that data — it's a direct relationship between your device and Google.

5 Children's privacy

Bruno's Alarm isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — see Section 1.

6 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
